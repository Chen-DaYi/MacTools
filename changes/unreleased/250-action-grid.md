Added Action Grid, a configurable, VoiceOver-accessible pointer-first launcher that centers its middle cell on the pointer when space permits, stays fully visible near screen edges, and supports editable 3×3 layouts, exact empty-slot placement, nested folders, and drag-and-drop ordering with a native preview, clear move-or-swap targets, and continuous placement feedback. Its editor reliably reopens after dismissal and puts keyboard focus in action search, while shortcut presentation initially selects the center action or the closest available action. Repeated gesture and shortcut presentation safely reuses or recreates a single overlay without destabilizing application activation, and gesture release cannot click through to a newly presented cell.
