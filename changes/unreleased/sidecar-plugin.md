Connect nearby Sidecar-compatible displays with compact direct actions, including a clear switch flow that disconnects the known current display before connecting the selected one. Connected-status indicators, saved per-display connection and shortcut preferences, and a priority-aware shortcut to connect the first available display are included. Wired-only mode never falls back to Wi-Fi. Shortcut conflicts with macOS or other apps are reported immediately, and unavailable connection states are kept distinct from connectable displays.
