Connect nearby Sidecar-compatible displays with compact direct actions, including a clear switch flow that disconnects the known current display before connecting the selected one. Connected-status indicators, saved per-display connection and shortcut preferences, and a priority-aware shortcut to connect the first available display are included. Wired-only mode requests a wired transport rather than a Wi-Fi fallback. Automatic Sidecar controls remain available when wired-only transport is unsupported. Sidecar shortcuts now use MacTools-wide conflict validation, imported settings are normalized, and unavailable connection states are kept distinct from connectable displays. Terminal feedback clears after the panel closes or expires before it can become stale.
